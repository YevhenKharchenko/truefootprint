A Simple New Way to Puzzle

Tap-Based Jigsaw Gameplay

Unlike traditional drag-and-drop puzzles, Fun Puzzle Master uses simple tap controls.

  • Tap to select a piece

  • Move it into position

  • Complete the image gradually

It's simple enough that anyone can pick it up, but you still need to pay attention to get the pieces right.
